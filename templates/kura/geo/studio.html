Add a carbon / restoration asset

Turn the selected area into an MRV record: what was planted, when, and what was claimed. Every analysis you run on it becomes a monitoring point.

Screening estimates using conservative Tier-1-style factors (editable per asset). Credits are only issued by a registry (Verra, Gold Standard, Plan Vivo…) under an approved methodology with third-party validation — use this to prepare and evidence that process.

Location IDs & land register

A Location ID is minted from the boundary itself — SHA-256 of the exact shape → {{ cfg.id_prefix }}-XXXX-XXXX-C with a check letter. Same boundary, same ID; move a corner and it changes. Every allocation, transfer or dispute is chained by hash.
